# Changelog

## 7.0.0+71

### New

- **Try Kuber Pro free for 14 days.** New users get it when setup ends;
  existing free users can claim it once from Home or the Kuber Pro page. No
  payment, nothing to cancel. When it ends, your data stays and Pro features
  go back to their free limits.
- During the trial: "Pro trial · N days left" on Home, a "Try Kuber Pro"
  checklist that ticks itself as you use each feature, one tip at a time, and
  reminders 3 days before and on the last day.
- **New Income & expense chart** on Home and Analytics. One column per month:
  the higher of income and expense sits on top, so green on top means you
  saved and red means you overspent. Tap or long-press a month for the details, and switch between Bar and Line and
  between the last 7 days, 6 months or a year.
- **Home balance card:** tap income or expense to see that month's total and
  how it compares with last month. Tap again, or anywhere else, to go back.
- **Custom dashboards** (was Advanced History): build pages of charts and
  lists from any search, save them and open them from History. Monthly charts
  use the new chart.
- **Motion:** buttons, cards, lists, sheets and charts move smoothly. Pick
  Auto, Expressive, Subtle or Off in Settings, Look and feel, Motion. Gentle
  vibration for toggles and long presses can be turned on there too.
- **Quick navigation:** tap a page title, or long-press back, to see how you
  got here as a tree and jump to any earlier page.
- Loans that grow in parts: add each part as the bank pays it out, and pick
  the account it reached. Interest is charged only on what has been released.
- Interest before EMIs: pay it every month or let the bank add it to the loan.
  Kuber works it out day by day, the way banks do.
- Add to loan: record more money released, charges or unpaid interest.
- Loans can count interest by days, like most banks: each EMI's interest is
  for the actual days since the last one. New loans use it; an existing loan
  can switch from its menu, with a preview of the new end date and total
  interest first. EMIs you've already recorded don't change.
- Upcoming events has a Calendar: every bill, EMI, SIP, FD maturity, reminder,
  card due date and money lent or borrowed on a month grid. Tap a day for its
  list, long-press a day to add a reminder.
- A new first page: pick your language, restore a backup or import a CSV, and
  watch the progress as it runs.
- Search in More finds settings, accounts, categories, tags, money plans,
  people, card names, note titles and tools. Recent searches stay on your
  phone.
- Answers to common questions on the Kuber Pro page.
- New themes: Saffron, Sky and Ruby. New theme options: Expressive colours
  (tinted backgrounds, or turn it off for clean white and grey) and Pure black
  for dark mode on every theme.
- Every world currency, with search and an option to show the currency code
  instead of the symbol.
- Recurring transactions can repeat quarterly, every 6 months, or on any
  custom schedule like every 2 weeks.
- Loans, investments and recurring payments got a big upgrade: what you still
  owe and the interest in every EMI, value, gains and XIRR for investments,
  FDs that work out their own value every day.
- Join the Kuber community on Telegram from About or Feedback.
- Kuber Cards, Notes and Reminders can have their own icon in your app drawer.

### Improved

- Transactions recorded by a loan EMI, an investment or a recurring payment
  say "Auto-recorded from loans / investments / recurring", with a link to the
  source right under it.
- Kuber is smaller and works fully offline, including the fonts for every
  language.
- Custom dashboards scroll, sort and change charts much faster with years of
  transactions, and keep your place in the list.
- Settings is a short list of groups that show your current values, and More
  keeps only what you use most. Notifications has its own page and matches
  your phone's notification categories.
- Recurring transactions, EMIs, SIPs and backups run in the background, even
  if you close the app, and show a phone notification when they're recorded.
- Your chosen currency shows everywhere: accounts, budgets, charts,
  notifications and widgets.
- Tapping a notification opens the related page and item.
- Ask Kuber can explain Accounts and Kuber Cards, what's free, what's in Pro
  and each limit.
- Kuber Pro shows when your plan renews, or when it expires if you cancelled.
- Welcome is one page shorter, and the app opens straight to Home.
- The app lock and the Kuber Cards biometric are separate settings.
- Dev Tools (once unlocked) and Permissions are in Settings, under About.
- Every row in More and Settings has the same tinted icon. Clearer selected
  options in every theme, including Pure black.
- Calculators can fill in from your loans and investments.

### Fixed

- The Income & expense chart no longer repeats or cuts off the first and last
  month labels in Line mode.
- Restoring a backup no longer drops your Kuber Pro plan until the next check
  with Google Play.
- A crash when Horizontal swipe was set to row actions.
- Settings could say notifications were Off after you allowed them.
- Close loan did nothing; deleting a loan, investment or recurring payment,
  and Skip next or Pause, did not always refresh the screen.
- A loan with the bank's rounded EMI showed one extra tiny EMI at the end.
- View EMI schedule crashed for loans from demo data.
- Editing a loan could clear numbers you didn't change.
- The bottom bar fits on small screens with large text.
