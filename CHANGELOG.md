# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Your chosen currency now shows everywhere. Accounts, budgets, charts,
  notifications, widgets and every other screen use the same symbol and
  number format you picked in Settings.
- Recurring transactions can now repeat quarterly, every 6 months, or on any
  custom schedule like every 2 weeks or every 10 days. Rules on the 29th,
  30th or 31st stay on that day in longer months. Your existing rules keep
  their schedule.
- Tapping a notification now opens the related page and the item it is
  about, both inside the app and from your phone's notification shade.

## 5.1.0+53 — Kuber Pro launch

Kuber Pro is here. Unlock SMS Import, Advanced Analytics, unlimited Kuber Notes
and Cards, Reminders, automatic backups, Multi-currency, and custom accent
themes. Start with a 14-day free trial on the yearly plan, or go Lifetime once.

- Pro gating is now live across all 9 gated features. Kuber Signature theme
  stays free in light and dark; the other accent families are Pro.
- Restore purchases and Google Play promo-code redemption from Settings.
- Launch offers configured in Play Console are surfaced and applied
  automatically on the yearly plan.
- No account, no cloud. Cancel anytime through Google Play.
